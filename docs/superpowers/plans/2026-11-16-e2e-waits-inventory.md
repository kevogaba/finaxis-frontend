# E2E long waits — inventory (PR 3, Task 9)

Every wait in `e2e/` longer than Playwright's defaults (5 s per assertion, 30 s per test) and every fixed sleep, as of the start of PR 3, classified against the design's §5.5: a wait sized for a cold `next dev` compile returns to the default; a wait with a production reason stays, its reason stated.

Durations are from a full run on the production build at 4 workers (4 vCPU, 16 GB). CI ran that suite about 1.25× slower (9.7 vs 7.7 min), so a test keeps an explicit timeout only if it took more than 12 s here, sized at about 3× its measured time; every other test keeps at least 2× headroom under the 30 s default even at CI speed.

Totals: 272 numeric timeouts of 10–20 s (267 assertions, 5 response waits), 34 `FIRST_HIT` uses (20 as an option, 14 as a helper argument), 39 test-group or test timeouts, one helper default and one deliberate sleep.

Named constants: `FIRST_HIT = 45000` in `overview.spec.ts`, `text-resize.spec.ts` and `users-invite.spec.ts` (39 uses, all "a route's first hit compiles under a cold `next dev`") → removed with their uses. `MENU_SETTLE_LIMIT_MS = 5000` (`e2e/support/auth.ts`) bounds a menu's grow-in animation at the default; it stays.
